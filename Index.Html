Are solar panels worth it in Yorkshire?

Solar panels can be worthwhile in Yorkshire because they generate electricity from daylight rather than requiring direct sunshine. Output varies with weather and daylight levels throughout the year.

How long do solar panels last?

Quality solar panels are designed to operate for decades and commonly include long performance warranties. Their output gradually decreases over time, but panels can continue producing electricity well beyond their initial warranty period.

Regular monitoring can help identify performance issues early. Other components, such as inverters and batteries, may have different expected lifespans and warranty periods.

Do I need planning permission for solar panels in Yorkshire?

Many domestic solar installations can be covered by permitted development rights when specific conditions are met. However, planning requirements can differ depending on the property and location.

Listed buildings, conservation areas and other properties may have additional restrictions. Your installer should consider the relevant planning requirements before work begins.

How long does solar panel installation take?

Many domestic solar installations can be completed within a relatively short timeframe once the design and preparation work are finished. The exact duration depends on system size and property complexity.

Roof access, electrical work and additional equipment can affect installation time. We can provide a clearer estimate after assessing your property and proposed system.

Can I get a battery with my solar panels?

Yes, solar panels can be installed alongside battery storage. Batteries store surplus electricity generated during the day for use when solar production is lower.

This can help households use more of their own renewable electricity, particularly during evenings. We can assess your energy habits and recommend suitable battery capacity.

What finance options are available for solar panels in Yorkshire?

What is the Smart Export Guarantee and how does it work?

The Smart Export Guarantee, or SEG, allows eligible customers to receive payments from participating energy suppliers for renewable electricity exported to the grid. Solar electricity used directly at home is separate from exported electricity.

Payment rates and eligibility vary between suppliers and can change over time. If you generate surplus electricity, we can explain the general export process and relevant considerations.
